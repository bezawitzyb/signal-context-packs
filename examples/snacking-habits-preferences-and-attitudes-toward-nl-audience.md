### Creative brief
- **Objective:** Launch a snack brand in the Netherlands by earning a first trial from 18-40 snackers. Do it through honest, label-level proof, taste and crunch, and fair price, not through big claims.
- **Audience:** Dutch snackers aged 18-40 who buy everyday sweet and savoury snacks. Some check kcal and sugar themselves, some are loyal to their paprika or bolognese chip, and some wait for a reviewer to try the new product first.
- **Insight:** They want snacks they feel better about, but they have been burned by 'healthy' labels and by snacks that taste like karton. So they check the numbers themselves and trust the verdict of someone who tried it before them. (Observation from the pack, not a proven fact.)
- **Message:** Zie wat erin zit, proef het zelf: een snack met een eerlijk etiket die ook echt lekker is.
- **Tone:** Casual, direct, funny. Short verdicts, mock outrage, emoji, plain Dutch with slang ('lekker', 'top', 'gatverdamme'). On TikTok, sceptical and number-first.
- **Mandatories:** Name the concrete flavour or snack (paprika, bolognese, frikandelbroodje); Say what the taste and crunch are like; Show the real numbers: kcal, sugar, price; Use 'nieuwe producten uit de supermarkt' and 'proberen' naturally; Use the language of nostalgia (jeugdsentiment) only when the story is truly about a lost favourite
- **Avoid:** Vague 'healthy' claims without ingredient and calorie facts; Promising less salt without proving the taste holds; Treating any snack as universally loved: bami, eierbal and kaassoufflé divide people; Pricing that is high with no reason given; Formal or corporate wording
