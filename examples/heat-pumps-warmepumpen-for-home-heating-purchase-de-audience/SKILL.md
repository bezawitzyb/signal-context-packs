Their words: WP = Wärmepumpe, heat pump; SCOP = Calculated seasonal coefficient of performance from the data sheet; JAZ = Jahresarbeitszahl, the seasonal performance factor measured over a year; Heizlast = Heat load of the building, used to size the pump; Zählerschrank = Meter cabinet, an extra cost item; Eigenleistung = DIY labour done by the owner; COP = Instantaneous efficiency ratio of the heat pump; Vorlauftemperatur = Flow temperature of the heating water; BWWP = Brauchwasser-Wärmepumpe, hot water heat pump; Heizungsbauer = Heating installer; Takten = Short cycling of the heat pump

## Guardrails
Say: Angebot mit Einzelpositionen; Vergleichsangebote einholen; örtlicher Installateur; reale Werte / JAZ; Vorlauftemperatur; Heizlastberechnung; Amortisation
Not: Advertising a best-case SCOP without winter values; Quoting only a total price; Promising that prices fall when subsidies shrink; Presenting a big national provider as the trusted choice
Never claim: 55 °C Vorlauf? Da läuft keine WP.; Wann ist das Geld wieder drin?; JAZ statt Prospekt; nicht nur, was der Hersteller verspricht; Günstiger Nachtstrom oder besserer COP; transparent cost and savings case; measured consumption and efficiency figures; woran erkennst du schwarze Schafe; subsidy rules differing by manufacturing origin; SCOP bis 4,7, im Winter eher 2,7
Sensitive: Heat pumps and the Heizungsgesetz are politically charged: posts frame them as a culture war or as a government scandal. Brand messaging that takes a side may trigger backlash.; Naming or comparing national providers like Enpal or 1komma5 can feed existing distrust. Claims that sound too good (halved electricity cost, all-in prices) are already met with suspicion.; Efficiency claims such as SCOP or JAZ figures are doubted by owners, who point to winter values and measured data. Quoting brochure numbers risks being mocked.; Subsidy answers must be right: owners ask about eligibility and proofs, and a wrong or outdated answer could harm trust, as the rules are said to be changing.; Price talk is sensitive: offers are called absurd and subsidies are blamed for high prices, so a brand pointing to subsidies or high quotes may look like part of the problem.; Several posts are Dutch-language and from the Dutch market and single posts back some themes (lifespan, noise, comfort), so applying them to German homeowners may be a misreading.
