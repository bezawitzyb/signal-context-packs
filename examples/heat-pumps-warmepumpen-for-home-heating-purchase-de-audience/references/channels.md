# Channels

1. facebook: Largest share of the conversation (140 posts) and the place where owners post quotes and ask groups for an assessment. Evidence mostly comes from these three platforms; others (e.g. reddit, instagram, tiktok, linkedin) are a test, not proven. Formats: text_post, carousel, poll. Where: Public German Wärmepumpe-Erfahrungen groups, Heizungstausch / Altbau groups. Tone: Direct and practical, price figures and quote questions, informal German; an occasional emoji is fine, no hype. (CHN-01)
2. x: Technical, measurement-minded owners compare metered data; good for JAZ, PV and Kernwinter discussions with the data-driven segment. Formats: text_post, thread, chart screenshot. Where: #Wärmepumpe, #JAZ, #Heizlast, #PV. Tone: Matter-of-fact, numbers first (kWh, °C, JAZ), show how the value was measured. (CHN-02)
3. youtube: Comments react to videos by questioning efficiency figures and asking for experiences, an opening for real-world explainers on radiators and flow temperature. Smaller volume (22 posts), so treat as a test. Formats: short_video, explainer with measured data. Where: #Wärmepumpe Altbau, #Vorlauftemperatur, #Wärmepumpe Erfahrung. Tone: Plain and sober, show the display or meter, admit limits. (CHN-03)

## How each platform sounds
- facebook (140 posts): Direct and practical, with price figures, quote questions and some emoji and exclamation marks. Here posters share offers and ask the group whether the price is fair; costs, itemisation and PV in winter come up most.
- x (27 posts): Technical and measurement-minded, with owners comparing their own data. Owners talk about metering, JAZ and SCOP gaps and timing the heating, rather than offers and prices.
- youtube (22 posts): Short comments reacting to a video, questioning efficiency figures and asking for experiences. Comments focus on whether a shown JAZ is good enough and on which flow temperature others run in old houses.

## Public communities and creators
- Public German Facebook groups on Wärmepumpe Erfahrungen / Heizungstausch (search and pick open groups) (community, facebook)
- Owners posting metered heat pump data on X (#Wärmepumpe, #JAZ) (community, x)
- Comment sections of public heat pump videos on YouTube (e.g. manufacturers like Vaillant, Viessmann) (community, youtube)
- Home Assistant Community forum (heat pump monitoring) (community, web_forum)
