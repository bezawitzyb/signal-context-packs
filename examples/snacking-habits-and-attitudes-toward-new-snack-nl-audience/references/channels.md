# Channels

1. tiktok: Short, playful haul, taste-test and poll formats are the clearest pattern here, with a local angle (Dutch snacks) and concrete products. Most of the evidence comes from TikTok and YouTube, so the other platforms are tests that follow. Formats: short_video taste test, snack haul/unboxing, YAY of NAY poll. Where: #snackhaul, #candyreview, #snacktip, #nieuweproducten. Tone: Playful and excited, with emoji and exclamation marks. Say 'lekker', not diet language. Never scold what is in a haul. (CHN-01)
2. youtube: YouTube has the largest volume (27 posts) of warm, chatty taste-test and vlog content, and titles name a clear premise. Formats: taste-test creator seeding, YouTube Shorts, longer 'nieuwe producten testen' video. Where: #candyreview, #snacks, #tasteTest. Tone: Warm and chatty. Use clear title premises such as a store plus a product, with emoji and tags. (CHN-02)
3. instagram: Brand-style captions about protein, hidden sugar and trainer snack tips show a way to explain a benefit. It is a test channel with less volume behind it than TikTok and YouTube. Formats: carousel, reel, snacktip post. Where: #snacktip, #tussendoortjes. Tone: Upbeat coaching tone, simple swaps that are easy to copy, with numbers shown but no calorie-counting. (CHN-03)
4. reddit: Short, relatable Dutch confessions and nostalgia questions get engagement, and this is where doubts about 'healthy' and snack nostalgia surface. It is a listening and conversation channel, so test it before you push content. Formats: text_post question, comment replies. Where: r/thenetherlands. Tone: Casual, honest, short. Join in before posting and never advertise. (CHN-04)

## How each platform sounds
- youtube (27 posts): Warm, chatty commenter talk ("Leuke vlog weer😊") next to longer video titles and descriptions about testing products. Here the talk is about test videos that mix healthy, protein-rich and unusual products, and about packaging and flavours of a brand shown in vlogs.
- tiktok (15 posts): Playful and short, with emojis and hashtags; excited about hauls ("Meeega snack unboxing!!!"). Hauls and unboxings are framed as fun in themselves, and Dutch nostalgia snacks come up through a haul comment and short posts.
