# snack buying and eating habits and attitudes toward new snack brands in the Netherlands - NL audience context

Goal (positioning): The evidence points to one open space: a snack that is tasty and healthy, with the label shown openly, while the audience's doubts about additives, repackaging and price stay in view (observation, not proven fact; HYP-01 is inconclusive, so test this side before committing).
Goal (content plan): The audience discovers snacks through short 'look what's new' and taste-test videos on TikTok and YouTube, so the content plan should be built on those formats with a verdict and an open question.

## Do first
- Film a TikTok 'taste test' in which your snack sits next to two supermarket snacks, with a label close-up showing the ingredient list and nutritional values, and a verdict in the first person. Only use the real label and say honestly what you do and do not know. (DO-01)
- Post a short 'Wat zit erin?' explainer on Instagram (carousel) and TikTok that goes through each ingredient and explains any additive in plain words (e.g. smaakversterker, e471), and answer every label question in the comments. (DO-02)
- Launch a pre-launch teaser series on TikTok with 'nieuw bij de supermarkt' spotting videos and a closing question ('Welke wil jij als eerste proberen??'), and cross-post a timestamped taste-test round-up to YouTube. (DO-03)

## Guardrails
Say: Lekker, makkelijk en gezond!; Een gezonde snack hoeft écht niet saai te zijn; handige snacks voor onderweg; Welke wil jij als eerste proberen??; nieuw bij de supermarkt
Not: Healthy framed as boring or a sacrifice; Calling something new when it is only repackaged; Ignoring price; one post says wait for the Bonus
Never claim: Een gezonde snack; Lekker, makkelijk en gezond!; welke snack je echt vult; Minder bewerkt én lekker?; Gezond én lekker, kan dat?; snack next to two supermarket snacks; our snack vs two supermarket snacks; test which snack fills you; Keeps you full; diet claims like vegan
