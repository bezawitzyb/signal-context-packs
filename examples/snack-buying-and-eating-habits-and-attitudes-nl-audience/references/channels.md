# Channels

1. tiktok: Most of the evidence sits here: spotting and taste-test videos, recipe videos on 'healthy is not boring' and first-try videos all draw attention and comments. The chatty tone with direct questions to viewers is the norm. Formats: short_video, first-try, taste test, label close-up. Where: #tastetest, #nieuwbijAH, #gezondesnack, #snacks, #hartigesnacks. Tone: Excited and chatty, informal Dutch with English loanwords (taste test, healthy, snacks), emoji, reviewer-style honest about price and taste. (CHN-01)
2. youtube: Round-ups and taste tests with timestamps help viewers decide what to buy and what to skip. This is a place for the longer verdict. Formats: supermarket round-up, taste test with timestamps. Where: #tastetest, #nieuwesnacks, #supermarkt. Tone: Honest reviewer, clear verdict (blijvertje or skip), timestamps for each product. (CHN-02)
3. instagram: Evidence here is only a hypothesis that taste tests and hauls drive trial among under-30s (inconclusive), so treat Instagram as a test. Label and ingredient carousels fit the label opportunities. Formats: carousel, reels re-cut of TikTok. Where: #gezondesnack, #snacktest, #nieuwindesupermarkt. Tone: Same friendly voice, with clear, readable label visuals. (CHN-03)
4. reddit: Only one data point (a nostalgic question about a snack that disappeared that invited shared memories), so this is a test. Use it to ask questions and listen, not to promote. Formats: question post, comment replies. Where: r/thenetherlands. Tone: Curious, no ad voice, honest about being a new brand. (CHN-04)

## How each platform sounds
- tiktok (19 posts): Excited and chatty, with emoji and hashtags, often speaking directly to viewers ("Welke wil jij als eerste proberen??"). New Albert Heijn finds, quick healthy snack ideas and homemade recipes are shown as short videos with hashtags.
