*speculative, 4 of 75 posts, observed*
- Kind: objection
- Answer: Fair, a box with things missing or wrong is annoying. From what people say, it's a logistics problem, not a food one. If you want to skip the risk, a week from staples you already have (rice, pasta, eggs, canned beans) is a no-delivery backup.

## OBJ-02: The kit vegetables sometimes arrive not fresh or rotten.
*speculative, 3 of 75 posts, observed*
- Kind: objection
- Answer: Rotten veg in a box you paid for is not it. It's an observation from a few posts, not a rule for every kit. If you're prepping yourself, prep the parts and freeze what you won't get to, so nothing goes off before you use it.
> i received 2 separate shipments that had bad produce (EV-0051)
> Sometimes veggies arent the freshest. (EV-0003)

*speculative, 2 of 75 posts, observed*

## OBJ-04: Meal prep takes too much of my week, and it stresses me out.
*speculative, 2 of 75 posts, inferred*
- Kind: objection
- Answer: Prep shouldn't eat your whole week. Prep components, not whole meals, cook a double batch and freeze half. Some people log 4-5 hours, so keep it to what you'll actually eat and skip the rest.
> that just stresses me out (EV-0021)
> I am not spending my precious weekend time in the kitchen. (EV-0022)
