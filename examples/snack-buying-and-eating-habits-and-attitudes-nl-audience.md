- **Audience:** Dutch snackers aged 18-40 who buy in supermarkets and online. They like to spot new snacks and taste test them. Some want 'minder bewerkt' and 'lekker, makkelijk en gezond', others want indulgence; both watch the price.
- **Insight:** They want to try new snacks, but they hesitate: is healthy really tasty, is it really new, what is in it, and is it worth the price? (Observation from emerging and speculative findings, not a proven fact.)
- **Message:** Lekker, makkelijk en gezond: een gezonde snack hoeft écht niet saai te zijn, en je ziet precies wat erin zit.
- **Tone:** Enthusiastic, conversational, informal Dutch with English loanwords, emoji and hashtags, short exclamations and direct questions. Reviewer-style honest, including about price.
- **Mandatories:** Show the real ingredient list and nutritional values; Use a direct closing question to viewers (e.g. 'Welke wil jij als eerste proberen??'); Say honestly what is new about the product; Be open about price; Frame anything that is not proven as an observation
- **Avoid:** Healthy framed as boring or a sacrifice; Calling something new when it is only repackaged; Ignoring price; Telling people to wait for the Bonus; Invented health benefits or product claims

### What performs
*So what: Content that gives a quick verdict, a discovery moment or a concrete recipe wins, so build the plan around these.*
- **Build a recurring 'spotted at Albert Heijn' series that frames each new snack as news, with a surprising flavour and a clear verdict on whether it's worth the price.** Discovery framing with a surprised tone and flavour lists made products feel like news. A surprising flavour in a familiar product drew curiosity and price comments. Round-ups that promise a buy-or-skip verdict help viewers decide. *(inferred)* [TKW-01; PERF-06, PERF-08, PERF-01]
- **Make healthy snack content as recipes or first-try verdicts that give a concrete reason to eat it, such as a fibre fact or staying full, and that show it isn't boring.** Recipes that tackled 'healthy is boring' and tied to a hunger-dip fact gave viewers a reason to try them. A personal first-try verdict of tasty, easy and healthy was relatable. *(inferred)* [TKW-02; PERF-02, PERF-07, PERF-09]
- **Make taste-test and seasonal lists easy to scan, with emojis, hashtags and timestamps, and end with a direct question such as 'Which is your favourite?'** Quick-scan seasonal lists tied to a familiar retailer worked. Timestamps let viewers jump to the products they care about. A closing question invited comments. *(inferred)* [TKW-03; PERF-03, PERF-04, PERF-10]
