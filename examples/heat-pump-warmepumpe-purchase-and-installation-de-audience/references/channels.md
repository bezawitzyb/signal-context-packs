# Channels

1. reddit: Reddit is the most active platform in this pack (62 posts). It is practical and technical, and posters share house data and ask for quotes and devices to be checked. The offer-check and Heizlast content fits here first. Formats: text_post, offer-check walk-through, AMA-style answer with house data. Where: Wärmepumpen-Subreddit with offer-check threads (check the exact name before posting). Tone: Informal, du-form, numbers first (build year, kW, JAZ, quote amount), no sales pitch, and answer with data. (CHN-01)
2. youtube: YouTube drives owner stories and a comment-section debate. One-year reviews with electricity use and cost-comparison videos with euro figures perform. Evidence is spread over three platforms, so treat the others as tests. Formats: one-year owner report, short_video, cost comparison with euro figures. Where: #Wärmepumpe, #WärmepumpeNachEinemJahr, #GasRaus. Tone: Calm and factual, with a curiosity-driven title. Keep the sarcasm about politics out of the brand voice. (CHN-02)
3. facebook: Facebook has short, helpful question-and-answer threads about application rules. Urgent news-style posts about KfW and quote-check posts get replies. It is the channel for subsidy-order content. Formats: text_post, carousel, short question to the group. Where: Facebook groups on heat pumps and KfW subsidy applications (public groups only, follow group rules). Tone: Short, easy-to-answer questions, clear steps, and an official source for any rule. (CHN-03)
4. tiktok: Only one item (a teaser that busts the 'Altbau geht nicht' myth and names an expert) supports TikTok, so this is a test. Run it for a few weeks and compare it with the three main channels. Formats: short_video, question-opening myth-bust with a stated build year. Where: #Wärmepumpe, #Heizung, #Altbau (only in posts that state the build year). Tone: Open with a question, name the build year, show one number, and keep it informal. (CHN-04)

## How each platform sounds
- reddit (62 posts): Practical and technical: posters share house data or a symptom and ask the community to check it. Offer reviews with house details and settings troubleshooting (flow temperature, heating curve) show up here.
- youtube (47 posts): Comment-section debate plus owner stories, with some sarcasm about politics. Here the framing is proof for the old unrenovated house: owner results, creators and a one-year bill.
- facebook (47 posts): Short, helpful question-and-answer replies about application rules. Subsidy mechanics dominate: application before work, invoice timing, and hydraulic balancing as a payout condition.
