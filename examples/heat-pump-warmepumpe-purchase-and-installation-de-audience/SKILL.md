# Heat pump (Wärmepumpe) purchase and installation decisions by homeowners in Germany - DE audience context

Who: German homeowners (35-65, owners of single-family houses, often older buildings) considering replacing a gas/oil heating system with a heat pump. Market DE, languages de, en.
171 public posts from about 156 people, mostly on Reddit and YouTube. This is what vocal people say online, not a survey of the whole market. Research from 10 Oct 2026.

Position: Erst nachgerechnet, dann gebaut: Heizlast, Förderung und echte Werte vorab. For: Hausbesitzer (35-65) mit alter Gas- oder Ölheizung in einem unsanierten Haus, die sich fragen: Klappt die WP bei mir, und ist das Angebot fair?

Goal (content plan): The audience asks for proof and checks (real owner figures, quote checks, subsidy order), so a content plan built on specific, checkable posts on Reddit, YouTube and Facebook fits what they already engage with.
Goal (sales enablement): Buyers arrive sceptical about price, sizing, old radiators and subsidy errors, so sales needs ready answers with a build year and a figure, plus a one-page subsidy order sheet. These are observations from a small sample, not proven facts.

## Do first
- Ask one existing installation customer, with the build year stated, to film a one-year-style owner report for YouTube and a TikTok cut. It should show annual kWh, the JAZ, the radiators in use and the flow temperature, and answer questions in the comments. (DO-01)
  Check with legal first: Customer testimonial from an installation customer must be labelled as advertising if connected, and one house's JAZ must not appear as typical. Safer: Mark as „Werbung/Kooperation“, state measurement method and period, add 'Werte gelten für dieses Haus'.
- Post an offer-check walk-through on Reddit and in a Facebook quote-check group, using an anonymised example quote with the amount. Show what to look for: Heizlastberechnung, sizing basis, buffer or Schichtspeicher, scope. Invite people to post their own quote. (DO-02)
  Check with legal first: Promotional posts in third-party communities without disclosure are hidden advertising, and quote data may identify a third party. Safer: Disclose the company as sender, use an invented or fully consented example, and avoid implying competitors' quotes are overpriced.
- Write a one-page subsidy order sheet for sales and customers, and share a short version as a Facebook post. The order is: application (BzA) before work starts, then the hydraulic balancing actually carried out (not just certified), then BnD. Use it in every first call, and point to the official KfW source for the rules. (DO-03)
  Check with legal first: Subsidy rules change and a fixed order sheet may be read as binding advice or guarantee of funding. Safer: „Orientierung zur Reihenfolge, Stand [Datum]. Maßgeblich sind die aktuellen Regeln der KfW. Keine Rechts- oder Förderberatung.“.

## What we heard (strength in brackets; only strong may be stated as fact)
- I'm trying to get the KfW application, deadlines and financing right. (moderate, 8 of 171 posts; SEG-01)
- My heat pump shows an odd flow temperature or cycling and I ask others what to change. (moderate, 6 of 171 posts; THM-06)
- The offers for my house and for other houses nearby came in EUR 10000 above the costs named in the video. (moderate, 6 of 171 posts; OBJ-02)
- I'm asking about or have done a do-it-yourself heat pump installation. (moderate, 4 of 171 posts; SEG-03)
- I'm confused about the order, deadlines and rules of the KfW application and afraid of losing the grant. (emerging, 9 of 171 posts; THM-02)

## Voice
Tone: Practical, question-led and informal (du/ihr). Posters give numbers and house details, and ask for checks. Humour and emoji are occasional (😂, 😅); one rant is in capitals.
Code-switching: Mostly German; a few English terms appear, e.g. 'do it yourself'. Abbreviations like WP, VL, HK, JAZ, BnD are used without explanation.
Their words: Altbau = old, often unrenovated building; Heizungsbauer = heating installer / tradesman; JAZ = Jahresarbeitszahl, seasonal performance factor; WP = Wärmepumpe, heat pump; BnD = Bestätigung nach Durchführung, confirmation after completion needed for payout; Kostenfalle = cost trap; VL = Vorlauftemperatur, flow temperature; BzA = Bestätigung zum Antrag, KfW approval confirmation; Geschwindigkeitsbonus = speed bonus in the KfW subsidy for replacing old heating; HK = Heizkörper, radiator; Heizkennlinie = heating curve setting; Kombispeicher = combined storage tank for heating and hot water

## Guardrails
Say: Use real figures (JAZ, kWh, quote amounts); Put the application before work starts; Balancing must be done, not just certified; Offer to show real owners' experience
Not: Calling any house an Altbau without saying the build year; Vague promises about savings without a figure; Treating the hydraulic balancing as paperwork only
Don't claim without a legal check: Wärmepumpe im Altbau geht nicht – echt?; ob dein Heizungsbauer ihn wirklich gemacht hat; Wir rechnen beides mit echten Euro-Zahlen durch; Dein Angebot liegt bei 35.000 €+?; Lieber 2000 € mehr ausgeben… Lohnt sich der Check?; Warum soll ein Haus 10 kW brauchen?; Ein Fehler im Antrag und die Förderung ist weg; echte kWh, echte JAZ, kein Prospekt; nur die WP: mutig oder einfach konsequent?; die nächste Kostenfalle?
Sensitive: Subsidy rules and deadlines appear differently in the posts (percentages, caps, KfW 458, KfW 358, timelines). Content that states them wrongly or without a date could mislead and damage credibility.; Posters are wary of cheap quotes and of big national providers, and one says they would never trust a tradesperson without a second opinion. A pitch that leans on low price or scale could backfire.; The Altbau objection appears mostly via creators rebutting it, and a commenter expects 17 degree rooms. Promising efficiency in unrenovated houses without sizing and radiator checks may be challenged.; Noise is dismissed by one owner and ruled out by another because of neighbours. Downplaying it could land badly with those who worry about a fussy neighbour.; Opinions on an extra energy consultant conflict, with one poster calling it unnecessary and another paying for a second opinion. A one-sided stance may annoy part of the audience.
