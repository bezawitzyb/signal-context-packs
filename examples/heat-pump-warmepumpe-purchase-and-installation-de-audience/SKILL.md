## Guardrails
Say: Use real figures (JAZ, kWh, quote amounts); Put the application before work starts; Balancing must be done, not just certified; Offer to show real owners' experience
Not: Calling any house an Altbau without saying the build year; Vague promises about savings without a figure; Treating the hydraulic balancing as paperwork only
