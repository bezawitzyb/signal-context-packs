**Creative brief**
- **Objective:** Build trust and enquiries from German homeowners with an older gas or oil heating system by showing, with specific figures and build years, that a heat pump can be checked before it is bought. The content serves the content plan first and sales second.
- **Audience:** Homeowners aged 35-65 in single-family houses, often with radiators and an ageing Gas- oder Ölheizung. They ask 'Klappt das bei meinem Haus?', post quotes with amounts, and want to hear from real owners first.
- **Insight:** They are not against the technology. They fear paying a lot for an installation that leaves rooms cold, an oversized system or a missed grant, and they trust peers' numbers more than promises.
- **Message:** Nachgerechnet statt versprochen: Heizlast, Förderreihenfolge und echte Besitzer-Werte, bevor du unterschreibst.
- **Tone:** Practical, question-led, informal (du/ihr). Numbers and house details first, occasional light humour, no hype.
