# Heat pump (Wärmepumpe) purchase and installation decisions by homeowners in Germany (DE)

*Made 10 Oct 2026 · Quick research · Goals: Content plan, then Sales · Posts from the last 180 days (100% of posts dated)*  
*Brief:* Heat pumps for homeowners in Germany  
*Audience:* German homeowners (35-65, owners of single-family houses, often older buildings) considering replacing a gas/oil heating system with a heat pump.  
*Online version with every post behind every finding:* https://signal-l2w5.onrender.com/packs/pk_n-QlY1xrqi0x

> 171 public posts from about 156 people, mostly on Reddit and YouTube. This is what vocal people say online, not a survey of the whole market.

## Summary

### For your goal: Content plan
**The audience asks for proof and checks (real owner figures, quote checks, subsidy order), so a content plan built on specific, checkable posts on Reddit, YouTube and Facebook fits what they already engage with.**
- How you'll know it worked: Replies and comments that ask for the figures table, the quote checklist or the subsidy order sheet, and people posting their own house data under your content.

### For your goal: Sales
**Buyers arrive sceptical about price, sizing, old radiators and subsidy errors, so sales needs ready answers with a build year and a figure, plus a one-page subsidy order sheet. These are observations from a small sample, not proven facts.**
- How you'll know it worked: Prospects who bring their quote or subsidy question into the first conversation and refer to the sheet or checklist you shared.

### Recommended position
**Erst nachgerechnet, dann gebaut: Heizlast, Förderung und echte Werte vorab.**  
For: Hausbesitzer (35-65) mit alter Gas- oder Ölheizung in einem unsanierten Haus, die sich fragen: Klappt die WP bei mir, und ist das Angebot fair?  
The doubt it answers: „Funktioniert das bei meinem Haus wirklich, und zahle ich am Ende eine Kostenfalle?“ Die Antwort sind Baujahr, Heizlastberechnung, echte JAZ- und kWh-Zahlen von Besitzern und die richtige Reihenfolge bei der Förderung. Es gibt keine Versprechen ohne Zahl.

### Your plan: do this first
1. **Ask one existing installation customer, with the build year stated, to film a one-year-style owner report for YouTube and a TikTok cut. It should show annual kWh, the JAZ, the radiators in use and the flow temperature, and answer questions in the comments.** Owners' own annual figures are the format this audience trusts and engages with, and they answer the 'will it work in my unrenovated house' doubt. *(effort: medium, impact: high; for: Content plan)*
   - How you'll know it worked: Comments asking for the figures table or the radiator details, and viewers posting their own house data.
   - *Check with legal first: Customer testimonial from an installation customer must be labelled as advertising if connected, and one house's JAZ must not appear as typical. Safer: Mark as „Werbung/Kooperation“, state measurement method and period, add 'Werte gelten für dieses Haus'.*
2. **Post an offer-check walk-through on Reddit and in a Facebook quote-check group, using an anonymised example quote with the amount. Show what to look for: Heizlastberechnung, sizing basis, buffer or Schichtspeicher, scope. Invite people to post their own quote.** Offer checkers already post quotes and ask whether they are fair, and the highest-scoring opportunities are the Heizlast explainer and the offer-check guide. *(effort: medium, impact: high; for: Content plan)*
   - How you'll know it worked: Readers posting their own quotes or kW figures in the replies and asking for the checklist.
   - *Check with legal first: Promotional posts in third-party communities without disclosure are hidden advertising, and quote data may identify a third party. Safer: Disclose the company as sender, use an invented or fully consented example, and avoid implying competitors' quotes are overpriced.*
3. **Write a one-page subsidy order sheet for sales and customers, and share a short version as a Facebook post. The order is: application (BzA) before work starts, then the hydraulic balancing actually carried out (not just certified), then BnD. Use it in every first call, and point to the official KfW source for the rules.** Fear of losing the grant through one mistake in the order or deadlines is a non-obvious theme, and sales can remove it with a clear sequence. *(effort: low, impact: high; for: Sales)*
   - How you'll know it worked: Prospects asking for the sheet or citing it in the first call, and short helpful replies from group members under the Facebook post.
   - *Check with legal first: Subsidy rules change and a fixed order sheet may be read as binding advice or guarantee of funding. Safer: „Orientierung zur Reihenfolge, Stand [Datum]. Maßgeblich sind die aktuellen Regeln der KfW. Keine Rechts- oder Förderberatung.“.*
