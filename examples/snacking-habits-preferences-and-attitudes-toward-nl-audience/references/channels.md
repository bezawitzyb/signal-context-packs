# Channels

1. web_forum: This is the largest evidence base (141 posts). It is where the strongest theme (missed snacks) and the strongest motivation (can't find the product in my supermarket) show up, in a blunt, joking voice. Formats: Discussion thread, Taste-test replies, 'Where to buy' answers. Where: Dutch food and snack forum threads on discontinued or hard-to-find snacks. Tone: Blunt, banter, forum smilies. Allow 'gatverdamme' reactions and don't get defensive. Be open about who you are. (CHN-01)
2. youtube: Supermarket-test series with timestamps are how people decide what is worth buying, and the comments are full of specific recommendations. This channel fits explorers and reviewer-followers. Formats: Creator seeding in monthly supermarket tests, Own taste-test episode with timestamps per product, Comment prompts asking for favourites. Where: Comment sections of 'nieuw in de supermarkt' / proefwerk-style videos. Tone: Warm, upbeat and curious, with emoji and compliments. Make the 'so you don't have to' promise explicit. (CHN-02)
3. tiktok: Sceptical, fast label-checking with calorie numbers is the native style, and novelty tips are tied to search tags. The evidence is smaller (22 posts), so treat the protein/gym angle as a test. Formats: Label check with kcal and sugar on screen, 'Nieuw in de supermarkt' tip, Blind taste test. Where: #gezondechips, #gezondesnack, #highprotein, #gym. Tone: Quick and sceptical, with 🙈/😂. Real numbers only. English is fine for label-checking creators. (CHN-03)
4. reddit: Evidence is thin here (two what-performs items), but 'why did it disappear' questions and short, emotional 'but it's so tasty' comments perform. Treat this as a test, since the strongest evidence comes from web_forum and YouTube. Formats: Question post about a missed or discontinued snack, Short, honest comments. Where: Dutch-language snack, food and supermarket communities. Tone: Relatable and short. Ask rather than sell. (CHN-04)

## How each platform sounds
- web_forum (141 posts): Informal, blunt and opinionated, with banter, forum smilies and strong reactions like 'gatverdamme'. Forum threads are where people rank snacks, defend chip flavours, grieve discontinued products and argue about snack-bar items and salt reduction.
- youtube (47 posts): Warm and friendly, with emoji and compliments on the video; creators sound upbeat and curious. Frames snacks as discovery: supermarket tasting series for new products, and comment sections full of regional pride in local specialties.
- tiktok (22 posts): Sceptical and quick, with calorie numbers, emoji and short put-downs; some creators speak English in a label-checking tone. Health is the main frame here: 'healthy' snacks are challenged on kcal and sugar, and value is questioned.

## Public communities and creators
- Comment sections of monthly Dutch supermarket-test videos (proefwerk-style, with timestamps) (community, youtube)
- Dutch food/snack forum threads about hard-to-find or discontinued snacks (community, web_forum)
- TikTok #gezondechips and #gezondesnack creators (public label-check and 'Nieuw in de supermarkt' videos) (community, tiktok)
- TikTok #highprotein / #gym snack recipe creators (community, tiktok)
- Reddit threads asking why a favourite snack disappeared (community, reddit)
