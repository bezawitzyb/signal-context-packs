# Channels

1. reddit: Short, blunt nostalgia and opinion threads draw replies, and a 'why is it gone' question post is a clear conversation starter. Evidence also comes from other platforms, but Reddit is where the vanished-snack and flavour-verdict talk shows up. Formats: question post, comment replies, flavour poll. Where: Dutch/Netherlands subreddits where snacks, food and supermarkets come up. Tone: Plain and relatable, short verdicts, no brand speak. (CHN-01)
2. youtube: Supermarket test series with timestamps are the clearest discovery route for new products, and commenters suggest specific items (even regional ones) for creators to test. Formats: creator seeding in supermarket test videos, timestamped taste test, comment replies with specific suggestions. Where: PROEFWERK-style supermarket test series, new-in-the-supermarket haul videos. Tone: Friendly and enthusiastic; compliment the video maker, name exact products. (CHN-02)
3. tiktok: Dutch comments here are sceptical and numbers-driven, and a 'new in the supermarket' short with health hashtags and a protein recipe short both show up as performing. Treat the TikTok volume (22 posts) as smaller evidence, so this is a test channel. Formats: label-reading short with numbers on screen, new-in-the-supermarket short, protein snack demo. Where: #gezondechips, #highprotein, #gym. Tone: Dutch: sceptical, numbers-first. English cut: label-reading, family-focused. (CHN-03)
4. web_forum: The largest share of the evidence (141 posts) comes from forums, where the voice is blunt and joking and the snack-bar and flavour debates live. Other channels beyond forum and Reddit are tests until the numbers are confirmed. Formats: thread starter, poll, reply to flavour and snack-bar debates. Where: Dutch food and snack forum threads on chips and snack-bar favourites. Tone: Short verdicts, jokes, the odd slang word; never stiff. (CHN-04)

## How each platform sounds
- web_forum (141 posts): Blunt, chatty and opinionated, with short verdicts, jokes and the odd slang word. Forum posts argue over specific flavours, brands and snack-bar items, including discontinued favourites and complaints about taste or texture.
- youtube (47 posts): Friendly and enthusiastic, with emoji and compliments to the video maker. Supermarket-test video descriptions and comments about new products, plus local pride in regional specialties.
- tiktok (22 posts): Sceptical and numbers-driven in Dutch comments, with a label-reading, family-focused tone in English posts. Here 'healthy' is tested against kcal and sugar figures, and ingredient checking is shown as a habit.
