**How to answer them:**

- OBJ-02: spoiled produce is the worst, especially when you're broke. freeze what you won't use in time ('frozen anything works') and check what you've got the day it lands.

- OBJ-04: you don't have to give up your whole weekend. prep a couple of components instead of full meals, freeze half, and keep the rest flexible.
- OBJ-05: liking the food and hating the lock-in are both valid. check how cancelling works before you sign up, or go DIY with cheap batch cooking and no subscription.
