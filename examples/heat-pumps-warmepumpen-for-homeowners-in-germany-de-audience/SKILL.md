## Guardrails
Say: Wärmepumpe or WP, in the plain terms owners use; Wartung nach Herstellervorschrift with itemised positions; Angebot with a clear Leistungsumfang; real JAZ values with context (brand, years, hot water included); Vorlauftemperatur and Heizkörper when talking about the Altbau
Not: Headline SCOP numbers with no real-world context; Lump-sum maintenance prices with no breakdown; Promising old radiators will 'just work'; Treating the heat pump as hard to understand when owners say it needs little effort
