# Channels

1. facebook: The largest evidence base comes from Facebook (32 posts): practical, sceptical swap-experience groups where owners ask for opinions, prices and local installer recommendations. Posts with a concrete offer or a specific installation failure perform best. Formats: text_post, question post with offer details, poll between two options. Where: Regional Wärmepumpe groups, Local groups for installer recommendations, Heizung/Altbau owner groups. Tone: Peer to peer, blunt and practical. Start with 'Frage in die Runde', give house data, no brochure language. (CHN-01)
2. youtube: The second source of evidence: DIY test videos, warning videos with chapters, cost-and-results balances and comments with owners' own efficiency calculations. Formats: chaptered explainer, long-term test or balance video, comment replies with figures. Where: #Wärmepumpe, #Altbau, #JAZ. Tone: Measured and numerical: name brand, years and whether hot water is included. Warning headline, honest result. (CHN-02)
3. web_forum: The supported hypothesis says owners rely on forum advice to judge installers and sizing. The pack has no direct forum posts, so treat this channel as a test, as with every platform beyond Facebook and YouTube. Formats: detailed question with house data, answer thread with itemised figures. Where: Wärmepumpe and heating forums (to be identified). Tone: Technical and sceptical; answer with numbers and context, no promotion. (CHN-03)

## How each platform sounds
- facebook (32 posts): Practical and sceptical, in a swap-experiences tone. Posters ask the group for opinions, prices and recommendations, and sometimes speak bluntly. Here the talk centres on maintenance quotes and what is in them, asking for local installer recommendations, DIY and brand noise comparisons. Posters often quote concrete prices or named brands.
