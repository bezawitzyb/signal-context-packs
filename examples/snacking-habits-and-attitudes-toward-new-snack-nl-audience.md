# Snacking habits and attitudes toward new snack brands in the Netherlands (NL)

*Made 10 Oct 2026 · Quick research · Goals: Positioning, then Content plan · Posts from the last 180 days (84% of posts dated)*  
*Brief:* Launching a snack brand in the Netherlands  
*Audience:* Dutch snack buyers aged roughly 18-40 who snack between meals, at work/study, and socially, including health-conscious and indulgent snackers.  
*Online version with every post behind every finding:* https://signal-l2w5.onrender.com/packs/pk_3I8-9eTZflyz

> 71 public posts from about 63 people, mostly on YouTube and TikTok. This is what vocal people say online, not a survey of the whole market. Early signals: fewer posts than a full pack needs, so treat findings as signals to test.

## Summary

### For your goal: Positioning
**The evidence points to one gap to own: a snack that tastes like a treat and shows, in checkable terms, why it is a smarter pick (observation, not proven fact).**
- How you'll know it worked: Commenters repeat 'lekker' and 'slimmer' in their own words, and ask for the sugar and protein figures.

### For your goal: Content plan
**The audience discovers products through taste tests, hauls and short YouTube and TikTok formats, so the launch content should live inside those formats rather than in brand ads (the evidence is moderate, and HYP-04 is inconclusive).**
- How you'll know it worked: Creators post the product in a taste test or haul, and viewers ask where to buy it or which flavour to try next.

### Recommended position
**Lekker, maar nét wat slimmer snacken.**  
For: Nederlandse snackers van 18-40 die tussendoortjes willen die lekker blijven, langer vullen en niet voelen als een compromis. Ze kijken eerst naar taste tests en hauls voordat ze iets nieuws proberen.  
The doubt it answers: 'Gezond' is vaak een label op iets suikerrijks en eiwitrepen stellen teleur. Het doubt is: kan een nieuw merk lekker én slimmer zijn zonder dat ik moet kiezen tussen genieten en me schuldig voelen? Observatie uit het onderzoek, nog geen vaststaand feit.

   - How you'll know it worked: At least one creator publishes a taste test or unboxing, and viewers ask where to find the snack.
   - *Check with legal first: Free samples to creators require clear ad disclosure, and comparisons with other products must be fair and not imply unsubstantiated superiority. Safer: Send samples with a note to label content as reclame/#ad, give an honest opinion, and avoid health or superiority claims about other products.*

**Top opportunity:** Educate skeptical consumers on why protein-rich snack bars qualify as healthy despite sugar content by showing front-pack nutrition transparently.
