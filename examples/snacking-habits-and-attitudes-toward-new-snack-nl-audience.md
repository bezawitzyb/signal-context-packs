**Creative brief**
- **Objective:** Introduce a new snack brand in the Netherlands as the lekkere snack that is nét wat slimmer, and get snackers to try it through taste tests and hauls.
- **Audience:** Dutch snackers aged about 18-40 who eat tussendoortjes at work or study, on the couch and socially. Some are fitness-minded and some just want to enjoy, and they all love finding new snacks by watching taste tests and snack hauls.
- **Insight:** People want to enjoy snacks ('maar het is zo lekker') without feeling they have to choose between enjoyment and feeling OK about it. They have learned that 'gezond' labels and protein bars often let them down.

- **Tone:** Casual, warm and enthusiastic, in Dutch with the usual English snack terms (snack haul, taste test, unboxing). Emoji and exclamation marks in social posts, and honest, practical answers where doubts come up.
